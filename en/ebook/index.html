E-book: The Path to Self-Acceptance, Self-Belief and a Freer Life with OCD

A short practical e-book (19 pages, A5 format, in Czech) by Bc. Václav Kodeš, based on my personal experience of OCD. It is not a guide to a cure or diagnostic material and it does not replace professional care. It is meant to give you a picture of how I think about OCD and whether this way of working suits you, before you decide to work together.

Download the e-book

The e-book is free to download, with no registration and without entering your e-mail.

Download the e-book (PDF)

PDF, 19 pages, A5 format, 5.3 MB. Click the cover to open it in a new tab.

What you will find in the e-book

  • You are not your OCD – the difference between an intrusive thought and who you are; self-acceptance as the opposite of resignation.
  • The OCD cycle – trigger, doubt, anxiety, compulsion and short-term relief.
  • Self-acceptance and self-trust – trust without guarantees and a look into your own story.
  • Compulsions and everyday freedom – how to recognise whether I am seeking certainty; a brief note on ERP and I-CBT, which belong with a professional.
  • Four practical worksheets and advice for when difficulties return and when to seek professional help.

Important: I am not a psychologist, psychotherapist or psychiatrist. Peer mentoring does not replace professional diagnosis or treatment.

Peer support is a complement to professional care. It does not replace psychotherapy or a doctor.
