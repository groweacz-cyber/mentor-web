For therapists and professionals

Peer mentoring is a complement to professional care, not a replacement for it. Here you will find what I do, what I do not do and how we can cooperate.

Bc. Václav Kodeš

Peer mentor with personal experience of OCD

Last updated: 23 October 2026

How cooperation works

Peer support is a complement to professional care. If the client is in the care of a professional, I am a support for them in everyday life. I do not interfere with professional care, I do not evaluate it and I give the client no professional advice. I tell the professional nothing without the client’s consent.

What I do not do

  • I am not a therapist or psychologist, I do not make a diagnosis and I do not lead psychotherapy.
  • I do not prescribe or deal with medication, and I never lead a client towards stopping it or ending care.
  • I do not give reassurance that a worry will not come true.
  • In an acute crisis or in a worsening I refer to professional help.

Whom I work with

I work with people who are in the care of a professional or are actively preparing to be. A diagnosis or a therapist is not needed for the introductory consultation, but for regular cooperation it is important that the client has professional care or is seeking it.

Contact for cooperation

Write to ocdmentor@gmail.com. In an acute situation please use the crisis contacts, not e-mail.

When to seek a professional

Contact a doctor or another professional if your worries and rituals take up a lot of time, limit your work, relationships or sleep, or if your condition is getting worse. Do not wait until it gets worse.

In an acute crisis or with thoughts of suicide or self-harm, call 112, 155 or the Czech first psychological help line 116 123. More on the page Help now.
