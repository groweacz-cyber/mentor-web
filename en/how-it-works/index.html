How it works

How a meeting works

The 20-minute introductory consultation is free and commits you to nothing. We get to know each other and see whether this kind of support makes sense for you. Further meetings last 50 minutes and take place online. It is a conversation with someone who knows OCD from personal experience: you can talk about what you are going through, I share what helped me, and I help you find your bearings in where to look for professional care. I do not provide psychotherapy, I do not diagnose, I do not advise on medication and I do not assign tasks. You set the pace and the topics.

How we connect: the sessions are one-to-one video calls on Google Meet or WhatsApp. You do not need to contact me yourself, after you book I will get in touch and we will agree how to connect. You can download the apps in advance, you will find store links and QR codes with each seminar, for example on the page The Path with OCD.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a peer mentor and a therapist?

A psychologist or psychotherapist is a professional who may provide health care. A peer mentor has personal experience of OCD and is a support alongside professional care: they share what helped them and help you find your bearings in where to look for professional care. I do not provide psychotherapy, I do not diagnose and I do not advise on medication.

Do I need a diagnosis or a therapist?

Not for the introductory consultation. For regular cooperation, though, it is important to be in the care of a professional or to be actively arranging it. If you do not know where to turn, I will help you find your bearings.

How does an online session work?

By video call (Google Meet or WhatsApp), from home or anywhere you have peace. A computer or phone with headphones is enough. You do not need to contact me yourself: after you book I will get in touch and we will agree which app to use. You can download the apps in advance, store links and QR codes are on the pages of the individual seminars.

How much does it cost and how do I pay?

The introductory consultation (20 min) is free. A session (50 min) costs 490 CZK, a package of two sessions 880 CZK. You pay online when you book.

Can I cancel or reschedule a date?

Cancelling a date at least 24 hours before it starts is free of charge. Rescheduling depends on availability and mutual agreement. If you cancel a paid session less than 24 hours in advance or do not show up without apology, the provider may keep the price paid as compensation for the reserved time. You can cancel or change the date directly in Reservanto or by e-mail at ocdmentor@gmail.com.

Is it confidential?

What is said in a session stays between us. The only exception is a situation where your own or someone else's health or life would be seriously at risk. I process personal data in accordance with the GDPR; you will find the details in the privacy policy.

When is peer mentoring not enough?

In an acute crisis, with thoughts of suicide or self-harm, severe depression or psychosis, care belongs in the hands of a professional. In an acute crisis call 112, 155 or the Czech first psychological help line 116 123.
