How much time does OCD take from me?

Rituals, checks, reassurance and thinking in your head are easily lost in an ordinary day. The calculator simply adds up the time you enter yourself. It is not a test or a diagnosis, and nothing is stored or sent.

Estimate how many minutes a day on average you spend on each area. A rough estimate is enough.

These are your own estimates, not a measurement or a diagnosis. If you are in an acute crisis, call 112, 155 or the Czech first psychological help line 116 123.

How it is worked out

Hours a week are calculated as minutes a day × 7 ÷ 60. Days a year are minutes a day × 365 ÷ 1,440, that is, whole 24-hour days. A weekend counts as two days. For example, 180 minutes a day (3 hours) is 21 hours a week, about 45.6 days a year and roughly 23 weekends.
