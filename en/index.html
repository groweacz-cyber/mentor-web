Why I do this

Since childhood I have gone through anxiety, social phobia and OCD in many forms. I know what it is like when your own mind holds you in check. I decided to pass on what helped me.

My approach and point of view

  • I myself lived with OCD, anxiety and social phobia for years, not from a textbook.
