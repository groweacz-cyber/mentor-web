Do you recognise yourself in this?

OCD keeps going round in the same loop. Click any stage to see what happens in it and how the method interrupts it.

  1. 1. Trigger

    What happens: Something sets off the alarm: an article, a message, a bodily sensation, a situation or a thought.

    What the method does about it: The trigger map and Roots. I understand when and why the fear is triggered, without blame and without endless analysis.

  2. 2. Doubt

    What happens: What if...? The mind produces a doubt and pretends it needs an answer right now.

    What the method does about it: Understanding (I-CBT). A doubt built on imagination does not need an answer.

  3. 3. Catastrophic scenario

    What happens: The doubt turns into a film about the worst possible ending. It feels real, but it is only an image.

    What the method does about it: Self-acceptance and self-trust. I do not know exactly what will happen. When a difficulty comes, I will look for a way to deal with it.

  4. 4. Anxiety

    What happens: The body sounds the alarm: pounding heart, tension, a clenched stomach, brain fog.

    What the method does about it: The body in fear. I name what I feel and let it be. The body speaks the language of anxiety, but it does not decide.

  5. 5. Compulsion

    What happens: A ritual, checking, reassurance, searching or avoiding. Anything meant to switch the fear off quickly.

    What the method does about it: Responding differently to the urge. The fear can be present and I still act.

  6. 6. Relief

    What happens: The fear eases for a moment and the mind records: the ritual saved me.

    What the method does about it: This is where the circle breaks. Relief comes from bearing the fear, not from certainty.

  7. 7. Reinforcement

    What happens: Next time the urge comes stronger and the loop spins faster.

    What the method does about it: Instead of "the ritual saved me" comes "I can handle it". Self-trust grows from that.

The loop can be interrupted at any point. ↺ and round again

How it works

  1. Free introductory consultation

    20 min, online

    We get to know each other, you tell me what brings you here, and we find out whether this form of support suits you.

  2. Intake questionnaire

    We map the themes, compulsions and the impact on your life. From that we build a ladder starting with the weakest theme and choose a path: Metal, Pop, or something in between.

  3. Regular sessions

    50 min, online

    A video call on Google Meet or WhatsApp. Steps between sessions, support and tracking progress. Alongside professional care, not instead of it.

More about the method
All 12 principles of the method
  1. Nothing in the method may work as protection from fear.
  2. A compulsion is an act of non-acceptance and of distrust in myself. The answer is a return to self-acceptance, not punishment.
  3. Self-trust is built through situations I get through. The fear can be present and I still act.
  4. Self-acceptance and self-trust will not stop intrusive thoughts, but they take away their power.
  5. An exposure text must not end with reassurance.
  6. The target vision describes a life, not the absence of an illness.
  7. I start where I have a chance to win and work towards what limits me most.
  8. I understand where my fear came from, without blame and without endless analysis. The story explains, exposure heals.
  9. The body speaks the language of anxiety. I learn to understand it, but I do not let it decide.
  10. Music recharges me, but it does not hide me from fear.
  11. I do not wait for OCD to ambush me. I expect it, and when it comes I greet it and carry on.
  12. Firm ground first, then understanding, then courage.

The loop that OCD uses

Tension leads to action, action brings relief and relief reinforces the loop. My work is to help you stay in it differently: with the tension, without the action.
