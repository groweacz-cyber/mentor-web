Intake questionnaire

It lets us get to know each other well and build a ladder of exposures together. It is not a diagnostic test. Fill in only what you want to share. You do not have to answer anything.

Nothing is saved. The site has no database or server. After you fill it in, you open an e-mail with pre-filled text, or copy the text and send it however you like.
