When OCD Lives Next to You: a guide for relatives

An individual programme of three 1:1 meetings. What helps people with OCD, what harms them, and how not to wear yourself out.

The seminar is based on my personal experience with OCD and on the perspective of someone who knows OCD from their own life. It is not professional or medical advice, psychotherapy, family therapy or a health service, and it does not replace the care of a psychologist, psychotherapist or psychiatrist. I am not a psychologist, psychotherapist or psychiatrist. At the seminar I do not diagnose, treat or advise on medication.

The service is intended for persons over 18 years of age.

3 meetings of 60 minutes, once a week on Tuesdays at 18:00 (Prague time), online 1:1. One more relative from your household can join the meetings at no extra charge. Price: 2,500 CZK for the whole programme. The price is final, I am not a VAT payer.

Do you live with someone who has OCD, or do you look after someone like that? You may feel that everything you try does not help, or even makes things worse. Well-meant help can sometimes keep OCD going without anyone intending it. In this programme I will show you how it looks from the inside, what helps, and how to keep your own life while doing it.

You can read more about the topic on the page When OCD Lives Next to You.

Three meetings

1

OCD from the inside

How the OCD cycle works, why a person cannot simply "stop", and why rituals make sense to the person with OCD even when they look illogical from outside.

2

Accommodation and reassurance

How we unintentionally feed OCD with goodwill. What helped me personally was when someone close to me, instead of reassuring me, showed that they could see I was afraid, that they were with me and that they believed I could bear it. This is not a guide or a recommendation for everyone. If your relative is in an acute crisis, call 112, 155 or 116 123.

3

Looking after yourself and boundaries

How to keep your own life, how to talk about professional help, and what to do when difficulties get worse.

How the meetings work

  • Just you and me, a video call on Google Meet or WhatsApp (whichever suits you).
  • I will contact you: once your payment arrives I will write to you by e-mail and we will arrange how to connect. You do not need to contact me yourself.
  • The camera is optional.
  • I do not record the meetings.
  • I do not share anything that is said with anyone else (exception: serious danger to life or health).
  • We can meet in English or in Czech, whichever you prefer.
  • I draw on my own experience with OCD; I am not an expert on family relationships.
  • I do not judge any specific person, I do not resolve family conflicts, and I do not decide for you whether to persuade your relative to seek treatment.
  • I will not teach you how to treat OCD, that belongs to professionals.
  • I do not promise that OCD will disappear if you do everything right.
  • Please do not share identifying details about your relative (names, diagnoses, specifics). General situations are enough.

Who it is for

For partners, parents, adult children, siblings and friends of people with OCD who want to better understand how OCD works and how not to feed it. The programme does not replace care for your relative or for you. If you are in a difficult situation yourself, please contact your doctor or a psychologist. In an acute crisis call 112 or the Czech psychological first-aid line 116 123 (in Czech).

Frequently asked questions

Does my relative have to know about the seminar?

No. If it is safe and appropriate, I recommend telling them about the seminar so they do not feel they are being talked about behind their back. The seminar is about your approach, not their treatment.

Can you tell me whether my relative has OCD?

No. I do not diagnose and I do not assess specific people. If you need to know, please contact a psychologist or psychiatrist.

Will I learn how to treat my relative?

No. The programme explains how not to feed OCD and how to keep your own life. Treatment belongs to professionals.

Can I come with my partner or another relative?

Yes. One more person from your household can join the meetings at no extra charge. I do not collect their personal data.

What if my relative is in an acute crisis?

Call 112 or 116 123 (Czech). A seminar cannot deal with a crisis.

Is this therapy?

No. I share my personal experience and my journey. I do not give professional or medical advice, I do not diagnose and I do not advise on medication.

How does a meeting work?

A video call on Google Meet or WhatsApp, 60 minutes, just you and me. Once your payment arrives I will contact you by e-mail and we will agree how to connect.

In which language are the meetings?

In English or in Czech, whichever you prefer. Just tell me when we arrange the call.

Do I have to contact you myself?

No. After you book and your payment arrives, I will get in touch by e-mail and send you the link to the first meeting.

Which app do I need?

Google Meet or WhatsApp. On a computer Google Meet works in the browser; on a phone or tablet you need the app. You will find store links and QR codes on this page.

Can I reschedule or cancel a meeting?

Cancelling or rescheduling at least 24 hours in advance is free of charge. If you cancel later or fail to attend without an apology, the provider is entitled to reasonable compensation for the reserved time in the amount of 50 % of the session price. The rest of the price is refunded within 14 days. In case of illness or for serious reasons (including an acute worsening of health), a meeting can be rescheduled free of charge even after the deadline. This does not affect the consumer's right to withdraw from the contract. Rescheduling is possible by arrangement via e-mail, at most once during the programme.

What if I get worse?

Please contact your doctor or therapist. You will find links and crisis contacts on the Help page. In an acute crisis call 112 or 116 123 (Czech).

I have not received the e-mail. What should I do?

Check your Spam folder and the Promotions or Updates tabs. Messages come from info@ocdmentor.cz and may land there. Mark the message as "Not spam". You also have the payment details on the page shown after you submit the booking. If you cannot find the e-mail there either, write to ocdmentor@gmail.com.

How do I pay?

After booking you receive a QR code and payment details. You pay by bank transfer within 3 days, and the date is held for you until then. If you pay from abroad, use the IBAN shown with the payment details.

What is included in the price?

All three meetings of 60 minutes. There are no other fees.

How is a programme different from single sessions?

Single sessions are free-form support based on the current topic. The programme is structured and consists of three connected meetings.

Are there meetings during the holidays?

No, there are no meetings on 24 and 31 December; the programme shifts by those weeks.
