Online seminars

Two individual programmes based on my personal experience with OCD. Choose the one that suits you.

For people with OCD

The Path with OCD: self-acceptance, self-belief and a freer life

Meetings based on my book about self-acceptance and the way to greater freedom, for people who live with OCD.

5 meetings of 60 minutes, once a week on Thursdays at 18:00 (Prague time), online 1:1.

3,590 CZK for the whole programme, the price is final, I am not a VAT payer

For relatives

When OCD Lives Next to You: a guide for relatives

How not to feed OCD with well-meant help and how to keep your own life, for partners, parents and friends of people with OCD.

3 meetings of 60 minutes, once a week on Tuesdays at 18:00 (Prague time), online 1:1. One more relative from your household can join the meetings at no extra charge.

2,190 CZK for the whole programme, the price is final, I am not a VAT payer
