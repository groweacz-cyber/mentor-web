The Path with OCD: self-acceptance and self-belief. Five meetings over a book

An individual programme of five 1:1 meetings based on the book. How I did it and what you can take from it.

The seminar is based on my personal life experience and my journey with OCD. It is not professional or medical advice, psychotherapy or a health service, and it does not replace the care of a psychologist, psychotherapist or psychiatrist. I am not a psychologist, psychotherapist or psychiatrist. At the seminar I do not diagnose, treat or advise on medication.

The service is intended for persons over 18 years of age.

5 meetings of 60 minutes, once a week on Thursdays at 18:00 (Prague time), online 1:1. Price: 3,900 CZK for the whole programme. The price is final, I am not a VAT payer.

The programme is based on a book that you can download for free: The Path to Self-Acceptance, Self-Belief and a Freer Life with OCD (the book is in Czech).

Dates

The programme always takes place on Thursdays at 18:00 (Prague time). The runs follow one another because I work with only one client at a time. Booking closes 4 days before the first meeting so that there is time for the payment and for sending the link.

What happens next: After you book and your payment arrives, I will contact you, you do not need to contact me. The one-to-one video call takes place on Google Meet or WhatsApp. Details and apps to download.

How the meetings work

  • Just you and me, a video call on Google Meet or WhatsApp (whichever suits you).
  • I will contact you: once your payment arrives I will write to you by e-mail and we will arrange how to connect. You do not need to contact me yourself.
  • The camera is optional.
  • I do not record the meetings.
  • I do not share anything that is said with anyone else (exception: serious danger to life or health).
  • We can meet in English or in Czech, whichever you prefer.
  • I do not reassure you with sentences like "nothing will happen to you" and I do not guide you through processing traumas.
  • The practical exercises from the book are optional reflection, not homework.

Who it is for

The programme suits people who are in the care of a professional or are actively arranging it. If you are looking for treatment or a diagnosis, please contact a psychologist, psychotherapist or psychiatrist. In an acute crisis call 112 or the Czech psychological first-aid line 116 123 (in Czech).

Frequently asked questions

Is this therapy?

No. I share my personal experience and my journey. I do not give professional or medical advice, I do not diagnose and I do not advise on medication.

How does a meeting work?

A video call on Google Meet or WhatsApp, 60 minutes, just you and me. Once your payment arrives I will contact you by e-mail and we will agree how to connect.

In which language are the meetings?

In English or in Czech, whichever you prefer. Just tell me when we arrange the call.

Do I have to contact you myself?

No. After you book and your payment arrives, I will get in touch by e-mail and send you the link to the first meeting.

Which app do I need?

Google Meet or WhatsApp. On a computer Google Meet works in the browser; on a phone or tablet you need the app. You will find store links and QR codes on this page.

Can I reschedule or cancel a meeting?

Cancelling or rescheduling at least 24 hours in advance is free of charge. If you cancel later or fail to attend without an apology, the provider is entitled to reasonable compensation for the reserved time in the amount of 50 % of the session price. The rest of the price is refunded within 14 days. In case of illness or for serious reasons (including an acute worsening of health), a meeting can be rescheduled free of charge even after the deadline. This does not affect the consumer's right to withdraw from the contract. Rescheduling is possible by arrangement via e-mail, at most twice during the programme.

What if I get worse?

Please contact your doctor or therapist. You will find links and crisis contacts on the Help page. In an acute crisis call 112 or 116 123 (Czech).

I have not received the e-mail. What should I do?

Check your Spam folder and the Promotions or Updates tabs. Messages come from info@ocdmentor.cz and may land there. Mark the message as "Not spam". You also have the payment details on the page shown after you submit the booking. If you cannot find the e-mail there either, write to ocdmentor@gmail.com.

How do I pay?

After booking you receive a QR code and payment details. You pay by bank transfer within 3 days, and the date is held for you until then. If you pay from abroad, use the IBAN shown with the payment details.

What is included in the price?

All five meetings of 60 minutes. There are no other fees.

How is a programme different from single sessions?

Single sessions are free-form support based on the current topic. The programme is structured around the book and consists of five connected meetings.

Are there meetings during the holidays?

No, there are no meetings on 24 and 31 December; the programme shifts by those weeks.
