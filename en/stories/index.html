Stories

Stories show that a person is not alone in this. Here you will find where my own experience is and under what conditions I publish other people's stories on the website.

Bc. Václav Kodeš

Peer mentor with personal experience of OCD

Last updated: 23 October 2026

My story

I have my own experience of OCD in several forms. I describe it briefly on the page About me. There is no longer story on the website yet, and when there is one, it will have no details of traumas, no claims of a cure and no advice on medication.

Other people's stories

I will publish another person's story only under these conditions:

  • I have written consent and the person can withdraw it at any time.
  • The story is anonymised: no names, places or unique details by which the person could be recognised.
  • It contains no claim of a cure, of results or any specific advice about medication.
  • I do not ask for stories or reviews in exchange for meetings.

There is no other story here yet. If you ever want to share your story, write to me at ocdmentor@gmail.com. I will not publish anything without your written consent.

Questions that can accompany a story

  • What did I come with and what did an ordinary day look like?
  • What was hardest for me?
  • What helps me now?
  • What would I say to someone who is just starting?

You do not have to answer all of them, and nobody is pushing you to.

When to seek a professional

Contact a doctor or another professional if your worries and rituals take up a lot of time, limit your work, relationships or sleep, or if your condition is getting worse. Do not wait until it gets worse.

In an acute crisis or with thoughts of suicide or self-harm, call 112, 155 or the Czech first psychological help line 116 123. More on the page Help now.
