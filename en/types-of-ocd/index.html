Types of OCD

OCD does not have just one face. Find the theme that feels familiar and read how it usually shows up. This is not a diagnosis; that is made by a professional.

Bc. Václav Kodeš

Peer mentor with personal experience of OCD

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Overview of OCD types

Health and body

Health OCD

Fear of illness, checking the body and seeking certainty that I am healthy.

Checking and responsibility

Checking

Repeated checking of locks, appliances and my own actions out of fear that I will cause harm.

Relationships, morality and meaning

Relationship OCD

Constant doubts about the relationship, the partner or my own feelings.

Cleanliness, order and rituals

Contamination

Fear of dirt, infection or chemicals and rituals around washing and cleaning.

Cleanliness, order and rituals

Symmetry and "just right"

A need for things to be exactly "just right", symmetrical or in the right order.

Intrusive thoughts and images

Aggressive intrusive thoughts

Unwanted thoughts or images of harming someone, even though I do not want that at all.

Intrusive thoughts and images

Sexual intrusive thoughts

Unwanted sexual thoughts or images that cause shame and fear.

Relationships, morality and meaning

Scrupulosity

Excessive doubts about whether I am morally in order or whether I have sinned.

Intrusive thoughts and images

Pure O

OCD with obsessions and rituals taking place mainly in the head, invisible from outside.

Health and body

Sensorimotor OCD

Constant awareness of breathing, blinking, swallowing or other bodily sensations.

Relationships, morality and meaning

Existential OCD

Insistent questions about meaning, reality, existence or death, to which I want a certain answer.

Cleanliness, order and rituals

Magical thinking

A belief that a thought, number or ritual influences whether something happens.

Checking and responsibility

Hyper-responsibility

An exaggerated feeling that I am responsible for making sure nothing happens to anyone.

Checking and responsibility

Real-event OCD

Doubts about real events from the past: did I do something wrong back then?

Health and body

Emetophobic OCD

Fear of vomiting and rituals meant to prevent vomiting.

Cleanliness, order and rituals

Perfectionist OCD

A need for something to be perfect or "just right", and endless correcting.

Relationships, morality and meaning

Confession OCD

An urge to confess or to check whether I have done something wrong.

Intrusive thoughts and images

Fear of going mad

Worry about losing control or sanity.

When to seek a professional

Contact a doctor or another professional if your worries and rituals take up a lot of time, limit your work, relationships or sleep, or if your condition is getting worse. Do not wait until it gets worse.

In an acute crisis or with thoughts of suicide or self-harm, call 112, 155 or the Czech first psychological help line 116 123. More on the page Help now.
